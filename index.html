About Our Academy

Providing a Nurturing Environment Where Talents Flourish.

Founded with a mission to deliver quality, value-based education in Mombasa, Beacon of Light Academy combines the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) with robust moral values, state-of-the-art learning aids, and sports development.

Our Vision

To be Mombasa's lighthouse of academic distinction, creativity, and high-impact moral values.

Our Mission

To identify, cultivate, and polish every learner's potential through tailored teaching and strong mentorship.
